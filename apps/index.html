Introducing the Momni Boards

Apps by Momni

Little tools for the big job of raising a family — chores, meal plans, date nights, grandma’s stories. The first Boards are almost here on the App Store. When they land, anyone can download them free — and Circle Up members get every single Board for a dollar a month, with Momni+ including them all.

The Momni math — Circle Up is $1. A Link is $1. Momni Gives is $1. And every Board? Also $1.

The Momni Boards

Built for moms, grandmas, and families — each one carries the elephant. App Store pricing is for everyone; member pricing belongs to Circle Up members and up. The first two Boards are almost here; the rest get built in the order the Circle votes.

How member pricing works

1

Download free

When a Board launches, it’ll be free to try from the App Store, for anyone.

2

Tap “Sign in with Momni”

One Momni account works across every Board — the same one you use in the Circle.

3

The price drops to $1

Circle Up members keep any Board for $1/mo (or $10/yr), billed through your Momni account in one place. Not a member yet? The App Store price applies.

4

Momni+ gets them all

Momni+ ($4.99/mo or $49/yr) includes every Board we make — no per-app charge, ever. And your Momni Gives $1 works across Boards too.

What “Powered by Momni” means

Every Board carries the elephant. Each one is built by the same small team, with the same belief: mothering is better done together. And every Board is a little doorway — someone finds ChoreBoard in the App Store, and on the other side of it, a whole Circle is waiting. Read the story behind the Boards from Karmel’s desk.

The Momni Boards (Apps by Momni) are built and operated by Momni, Inc. App Store pricing and availability are set per app; member pricing requires Circle Up membership or above, billed through your Momni account. The Momni Foundation (momnifoundation.org) is a separate 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization — one brand, two entities, separate finances.

Momni is a community platform. Members make their own care decisions. Care payments are made directly between members.
