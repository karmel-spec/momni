Connection – Momni Core Values

From the original Momni community blog — preserved from our archives so the Circle's earliest words live on. 💜

Connection—Linking mothers to ensure strength Sometimes, a simple connection means everything. They say it takes a village to raise a child and we agree! As mothers, we’ve been blessed with the most difficult job in the world, but we don’t have to do it alone . Momni encourages mothers to link together in raising children so that mothers everywhere might have the strength and support they need.

“We believe that mothers need mothers and that connections make us happier and stronger. We link into a strong chain of support through global caresharing technology and Momni circles.” –Momni Core Values Find moms who are ready to help! We’ve all been there. You have a million things to do, the baby was up all night, you’re exhausted and the kids are all screaming… at the same time.

It’s okay to need help! Circle up! There’s a mom in your neighborhood who would be more than happy to entertain your babies while you take care of life. The best part?

She’s likely listed as a Momni host and you can find her with the click of a button. Build a trusted circle! Momni circles were created to connect you with moms that share your interests! If you can’t find a circle that suits you, create one!

Become a circle host ! There may even be a fun gift in it for you and your circle… If you’re interested in becoming a circle host, REACH OUT to us at support@momni.com.

This post was written in the earliest days of the Momni movement and recovered for our living history. Read more in the blog or the Momni History timeline.
