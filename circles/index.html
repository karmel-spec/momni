What a Circle is

Friendship first. Care second. Always.

Momni isn't a directory of strangers. It's a community — and Circles are where the community actually happens.

Mamas who meet

A Circle gathers in real life — playgrounds, living rooms, library story hours. You see how a mama mothers before you ever need her.

Mamas who play

The kids become friends first. So when a care day comes, your little one isn't going to a stranger's house — she's going to her friend's house.

Mamas who trade care

Inside a Circle, care flows naturally — a swap here, a booking there, a rescue on the hard days. You choose your Momni from mamas you actually know.
