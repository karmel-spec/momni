March 16, 2017

The vision arrives 🐘

On a prayer-filled drive to Arizona — a spiritual retreat she'd promised herself — Karmel, a mother of eight from Provo, Utah, receives a calling she can't shake: build a sharing-economy answer to the global childcare crisis. Mothers already know how to care for each other's children; they always have. Make the village findable again. The first person she tells is her own mother — who believes her instantly. She names it Momni — Mom + omni, a mom for all — and everything on this page grows from this moment. (The whole story is in Circle Up, chapter by chapter.)

Momni is a community platform. Momni does not employ, supervise, or make any representation about any member. Each member is solely responsible for choosing care arrangements, exactly as they would when finding a friend in the neighborhood. Care payments are made directly between members; Momni never processes care payments.

momni.com and app.momni.com are operated by Momni, Inc. The Momni Foundation (momnifoundation.org) is a separate 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. One brand, two entities, separate finances.
