Newsletter · 4 design concepts

Same sample issue (June 2026, Issue 01) shown four ways. Pick the look that feels most like Momni — or mix them. The archive library below keeps every issue readable forever.

What stays the same every month (standard): the masthead, Karmel's letter, By the Numbers (live from the app), and the “light up your pin” invite.
What changes monthly (rotating): the seasonal color/theme, the featured mama story, the mama tip, and a Campfire highlight. So every issue feels fresh but unmistakably Momni.
CONCEPT A

The Circle Letter

warm, personal, magazine

A single warm column that reads like a letter from Karmel herself — intimate, story-first, the most personal. Best if the newsletter should feel like mail from a friend.

The Campfire Digest

modular, scannable, lively

Bright modular cards on a dark Campfire background — skimmable in 20 seconds, easy to add/remove sections each month. Best if mamas read on the go.

Heritage Postcard

scrapbook, handwritten, nostalgic

A keepsake postcard with washi tape, little polaroids and Caveat handwriting — leans into the heritage and the elephant-memory theme. Best if each issue should feel collectible.
