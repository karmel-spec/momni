Newsletter · Round 2 · The Ripple Round

Four new designs, all guided by one idea: a single drop of care ripples outward — droplet, rings, puddle, stream, river, ocean — until a community of Momnis becomes a tsunami of childcare. The goal isn't just to circle up. It's to circle up and circle out. Same sample issue (June 2026, Issue 01) shown four ways.

Yes — I can see it, and it's real heritage. Your December 2017 brand guidelines (page 11, “Patterns”) are built from overlapping concentric rings — raindrops on water — in Momni Purple and teal, “to emphasize the linking and connectedness that is Momni.” The ripple was in Momni's DNA from the very beginning. Concept B below revives the original pattern pixel-for-pixel; the other three carry the idea in new directions.

CONCEPT A

The Drop

quiet, elegant, one drop at a time

The most intimate take: a single animated droplet lands at the masthead and its rings carry you down the page. Every section is one ring further out — your story, your Circle, your city, the movement. Best if the newsletter should feel like a quiet moment, not a broadcast.

CONCEPT C

Watershed

droplet → puddle → stream → river → ocean

The storyteller: the whole issue is the water's journey. It opens on a single drop in lavender, and each section flows downhill — puddle, stream, river — the page deepening to ocean teal as the story grows, ending at the edge of the tsunami. Best if every issue should feel like a journey with a current.
