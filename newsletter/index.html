✦ This month's issue

Six years ago we pressed pause. This month, we pressed play. The map lit up with 25,373 of you — every first Momni, back on the map where they belong. I cried at my desk more than once, watching the lights come on across all fifty states. This is the first issue of The Circle, our monthly letter home. It'll change a little each month — but it will always start the same way: with you. Welcome home, Momni. 💜

A Momni story
“My pin is still on the map.”

April from Kearns, Utah hosted some of the very first Momni littles back in 2018. This month she became one of the first to relight her pin in 2.0 — teal, because she's a host. Welcome back, April.

Momni tip of the month
Meet first, always. A park, a porch, a playdate where you stay — let the littles tell you how it feels before you ever book. The best Momni friendships start with a no-pressure hello.

Momni is a community platform. Momni does not employ, supervise, or make any representation about any member. Each member is solely responsible for choosing care arrangements, exactly as they would when finding a friend in the neighborhood. Care payments are made directly between members; Momni never processes care payments.
