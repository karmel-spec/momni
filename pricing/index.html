What's a Link?

A Link is a single booking with a Momni — $1, every time. That dollar is the only thing Momni ever charges for care. The care itself you pay your Momni directly, and they keep every penny. Momni+ includes unlimited Links.

Hosting is always free

Open your door for free, forever. Set your own rate, your own hours — and keep every dollar of what you earn. Momni never takes a percentage of care.

Momni Gives 🙌

An optional toggle adds $1 to the Momni Foundation — a separate 501(c)(3) — to fund care for families with no options at all. 100% goes to the Foundation; flip it on or off anytime.

One brand, two wallets, fully disclosed. Momni, Inc. runs the app; the Momni Foundation is a separate 501(c)(3). Momni is a community platform, not an agency — we don't vet, screen, or endorse anyone, and your care payments are always yours, Momni to Momni.
