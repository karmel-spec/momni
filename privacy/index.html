Privacy Policy

Draft — June 2026

Momni is a community of mothers, and your trust matters to us more than anything we could ever do with your data. This policy explains, in plain words, what we collect and how we treat it.

What we collect

  • Account basics — your name, email, city, and what you choose to put on your profile.
  • What you choose to share — reviews you write and receive, photos you post, and any document (like a background check you purchased yourself) that you choose to display. These are your content; you control them.
  • Location — your chosen neighborhood or radius for the map. Precise location is never shown to other members; you decide how specific your pin is.
  • Payments to Momni — membership and Link purchases, handled by our payment processor. Momni never processes care payments between members and never sees them.

What we never do

  • We never sell your personal information.
  • We never show your exact address to anyone — you choose what to share, with whom, and when.
  • We never display legacy Momni 1.0 member information on the map. First-mama pins are anonymized at the city level until a mama claims her own pin and opts in.

Children's information

Momni accounts are for adults. Anything you share about your children (ages, notes for a host) is shown only to the members you choose to connect with, and is never used for advertising.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your profile content anytime, export your data by asking, and close your account whenever you choose. Deleting your account removes your profile and pins from the map.

Two entities

momni.com and app.momni.com are operated by Momni, Inc. If you donate to the Momni Foundation (a separate 501(c)(3)), your donation information is handled by the Foundation, not the company.

Questions

Write us anytime: momni.com/contact.
