How the giving works

Momni Shop gear is printed and shipped on demand — no warehouses, no waste, no inventory costs. After printing and shipping costs, 100% of the profit on every item is donated to the Momni Foundation, the independent 501(c)(3) that helps fund hours of care for parents in the world's hardest places.

Your baby’s onesie funds their care hour. That’s the Circle.
