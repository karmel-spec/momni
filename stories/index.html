The relaunch letter · From Karmel

Your pin is still on the map.

A letter to every mama who ever circled up — and every mama about to.
"I kept the map. Through every quiet year, I kept it. Fourteen thousand pins, each one a mother who opened her door or knocked on one. People asked me why I didn't take it down. Because you don't take down a lighthouse just because it's foggy, that's why. Mamas — the fog is lifting. Momni is back, and this time it belongs to no one but us…"
Karmel Larson · Founder of Momni · Provo, Utah
