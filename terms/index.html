Terms of Service

Written in plain English on purpose. If a sentence here would confuse a tired mama at midnight, we rewrote it until it wouldn't.

Draft last updated June 10, 2026 · Applies to momni.com and app.momni.com, operated by Momni, Inc.

1. What Momni is — and isn't

Momni is a community platform. We give mothers tools to find each other, get to know each other, and arrange care with each other directly. Momni is not a care provider, a daycare, an agency, an employer of caregivers, or a referral service. We do not provide, supervise, direct, or control any care.

Plain English: Momni is the bulletin board and the meeting place — not the babysitter.

2. No evaluation or endorsement of members

Momni does not evaluate, endorse, or make any representation about any member. We do not conduct interviews, inspections, or background checks on members, and a member's presence on the platform means only that she created an account — nothing more.

Plain English: Every mama on Momni is here because she signed up, not because we picked her. Getting to know her is your job, just like with any new friend.

3. You are responsible for your care decisions

Each member is solely responsible for deciding whom to meet, whom to trust, and what care arrangements to make. You agree to use your own judgment — meeting members, asking questions, checking references, and taking any other steps you consider appropriate — before entering any care arrangement. By using Momni, you accept and assume responsibility for the choices you make and the arrangements you enter, exactly as you would when choosing a friend or neighbor to care for your children.

4. Care payments are member-to-member

All payments for care are made directly between members. Momni never processes, holds, transmits, or insures care payments, never sets care rates, and never takes any percentage or commission on care income. Any payment dispute between members is between those members.

5. Momni's fees

Momni charges only the following platform fees, none of which are connected to care payments:

  • Free tier — basic membership costs nothing, and hosting is always free.
  • Circle Up — $1/month, billed annually at $12/year.
  • Links — $1 per new care connection ("Link"), sold in bundles, paid by the member seeking care.
  • Momni+ — $4.99/month or $49/year.
  • Momni Gives — an entirely voluntary $1 toggle that supports the Momni Foundation (see Section 10).

6. Reviews are member content

Reviews, ratings, and profile information are created by members and reflect only the views of the members who wrote them. Momni does not evaluate the accuracy of reviews and does not adopt or endorse them. You agree that any review you write will be honest and based on your own experience.

7. Background checks are member content

Some members choose to purchase a background check from a third-party provider and choose to display it on their profile. A displayed background check is member-purchased, member-displayed content. Momni does not order, review, validate, or interpret background checks, makes no representation about their accuracy or completeness, and does not require them. Deciding whether and how to weigh a displayed background check is entirely up to you.

The Momni Foundation (momnifoundation.org) is a separate 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization with separate finances and governance. Momni Gives contributions go to the Foundation, not to Momni, Inc. One brand, two entities, separate wallets.

When this draft is finalized and whenever it materially changes, we will tell you plainly — by email and on this page — before changes take effect.
