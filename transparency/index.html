the most honest page we know how to write —

Both things are true.

Momni is real hope: Momnis linking together, circling up, carrying each other the way mothers always have. And Momni makes no promises: no checks, no assurances, no illusions of a perfect world. This page holds both truths side by side — on purpose, in plain English.

Last updated June 2026 · Applies to momni.com and app.momni.com, operated by Momni, Inc.

We believe mothers are the solution to the childcare crisis.
We also believe no platform can ever be the filter between your child and the world.
Hold both. That's Momni.

MOMNI IS

A community of Momnis

A place to widen your neighborhood — to find the Momni four streets over you'd never have met, and to circle up the way mothers always have. The village, made findable again.

MOMNI IS NOT

A childcare agency

Not a daycare, not a nanny service, not a referral company. Nobody on Momni works for Momni. Every Momni here is a neighbor, not an employee — and a profile means one thing only: they signed up.

MOMNI BELIEVES

Mothers are the solution

Millions of mothers already know how to love, feed, comfort and protect little ones. We believe linking them together is the most powerful answer to the childcare crisis the world has ever had.

MOMNI DOES NOT BELIEVE

In a perfect world

We make no promise of any kind, and we sell no illusion. No platform on earth can promise you a person. Anyone who says otherwise is selling something we refuse to sell.

MOMNI GIVES

The meeting place and the tools

Profiles in their own words. A map to find them. Messages to know them. Links to arrange care. Reviews where Momnis speak to Momnis. A Circle to belong to.

MOMNI DOESN'T GIVE

Approval, endorsement, or assurance

We don't check anyone out, we don't pick anyone for you, and we never stand behind any member. We built it this way on purpose — because real trust can't be outsourced.

MOMNI SHOWS

What members choose to share

Reviews are Momnis' own opinions of their own experiences. A background check on a profile is a report they purchased themselves and chose to display — their content, shown their way.

MOMNI DOESN'T CONFIRM

Any of it

We don't review, evaluate, or adopt what members share — not reviews, not references, not reports. Weigh it all the way you'd weigh a neighbor's word: with your own eyes and your own judgment.

MOMNI HANDLES

Small platform fees, openly

Memberships from $1. A dollar per booking Link. Priced on a public page, never hidden, never a surprise.

MOMNI NEVER TOUCHES

Care money

What you pay a Momni is between you and them — directly, fully, forever. No processing, no holding, no percentage, no commission. Their $8 an hour is $8 an hour.

MOMNI CAN

Listen, and act on reports

Every profile and Link has a report button that reaches a human. We can remove members and close accounts, and we always will when the Circle's wellbeing asks it.

MOMNI CANNOT

Be in the room

We cannot supervise a visit, watch a home, or stand beside your child. In a real emergency or a possible crime, call 911 and your local authorities first — always — then tell us.

so whose job is it?

Yours, Momni. Entirely, exclusively yours.

The decision of who comes near your children belongs to you and you alone — not to us, not to an algorithm, not to a star rating. We say this in big type because you deserve a platform that respects you enough to tell you the truth:

Momni provides no checks, no approvals, no promises and no assurances about any member, home, or arrangement. The responsibility for choosing care — and for keeping your children protected — rests solely with parents.

That isn't us stepping away from you. It's us refusing to pretend. Because the moment a platform claims it can stand between your child and every risk, it has already lied to you once.

You are the filter. The Circle is your toolbox.

What Momni can't do for you, Momnis do for each other — in person, over time, with eyes wide open. This is how the Circle builds its own trust:

1
Meet first

A park, a porch, a playdate where you stay. Always meet before you book.

2
Ask everything

Discipline, screens, pets, who else is home, naps, allergies. A good Momni loves the questions.

3
Check references

Ask for them — then actually call them. Members can also share reports they purchased themselves; weigh them with your own judgment.

4
Read the reviews

Momnis speaking to Momnis about their own experience — useful word-of-mouth, never our endorsement.

5
Start small

One hour nearby before a full day. Let the littles tell you how it feels.

6
Trust your gut

If anything feels off, it's a no — no explanation owed, ever.
