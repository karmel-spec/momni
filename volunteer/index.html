Volunteer with Momni

Momni 2.0 runs with no employees and no investors — just one founder, helpful technology, and mamas who give an hour where they can. Here's where hands are needed most.

💜

Circle Leader

Start or lead a local Circle — host the playgroup, welcome new mamas, keep the rhythm. The most important volunteer role in the whole movement.

2–4 hrs/month
👋

Welcome Team

Be the first hello. Reach out to new mamas joining in your area, answer their questions, and help them feel at home in the Circle.

1–2 hrs/week
📣

First-Mama Finder

Know mamas from Momni 1.0? Help us light up the map — invite them back, one pin at a time.

Whenever you can
✍️

Story Collector

Gather and share Circle stories for the blog and socials — the night-shift saves, the first Links, the friendships.

1–2 hrs/week
🐘

Foundation Hands

Help the Momni Foundation with Circle fundraisers, the annual drive, and hour-for-hour outreach.

Seasonal
🧪

Beta Tester

Use the app early, break things kindly, and tell us what a real mama actually needs. Phone + opinions required.

30 min/week

Next steps

  1. Join the Circle — if you checked "Volunteer" on the waitlist, you're already on the volunteer list. 💜
  2. Email us at hello@momni.com with the role that pulls at you and your city — Karmel personally reads every one.
  3. You'll hear back with your starting point — Circle leaders get the Leader Kit, the Welcome Team gets its first introductions, beta testers get the app.
